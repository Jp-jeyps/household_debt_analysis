# 🔎 Preliminary Analysis

The dataset was initially examined using descriptive statistics to assess the **distribution** and **variability** of the variables.

## 📊 Descriptive Statistics

The following measures were calculated:

**Mean, median, minimum, maximum, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation.**

They provided an initial overview of the dataset and allowed the variables to be compared in terms of their **relative variability**.

---

## 📈 Variability Screening

The coefficient of variation was then used to compare the **relative variability** of the explanatory variables and identify those with insufficient variation for further analysis.

### Variables excluded:

❌ **X1** — Average unemployment rate  
❌ **X4** — Consumer Price Index  
❌ **X6** — Average interest rate on consumer loans  
❌ **X13** — Average APRC

This reduced the initial set of explanatory variables before moving on to correlation analysis.

📈 **Visual:** **Coefficient of Variation Ranking** — a horizontal bar chart comparing the coefficient of variation across the explanatory variables and highlighting those excluded at this stage.
